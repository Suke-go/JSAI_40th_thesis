# Survey Search Plan

## 1. Recommender Systems and Personalization
- Identify major review articles covering evolution from collaborative filtering to deep learning, sequence-aware recommenders, context-aware and knowledge-graph recommenders.
- Collect studies addressing user modeling beyond preferences: affective, contextual, long-term user modeling.
- Gather critiques or limitations related to cold-start, lack of semantic understanding, absence of autobiographical grounding.

## 2. Autobiographical Memory, Narrative Identity, and Technology
- Review foundational psychological literature: autobiographical memory frameworks, narrative identity formation, reminiscence therapy.
- Find HCI/Human-centered computing works focusing on memory support, personal informatics reflection, lifelogging.
- Search for empirical studies measuring impact of digital tools on memory recall and self-narration.

## 3. Relationship- and Body-Centered Interventions
- Summarize literature on social/relational robotics, telepresence, therapeutic devices (PARO, Telenoid), include critiques on efficacy.
- Investigate somaesthetic design, embodied interaction critiques, cross-cultural considerations.

## 4. First-Person and Autoethnographic Research
- Collect Japanese and international scholarship on 一人称研究／first-person inquiry, focusing on works by 諏訪正樹、松原正樹らの系譜とHCI領域におけるautoethnographyやautobiographical designの事例。
- Capture both擁護的な議論（濃密な省察価値、設計知への寄与）と批判的議論（普遍化の困難さ、再現性・妥当性の懸念、倫理的課題）。
- 主要キーワード：一人称研究、first-person research、autoethnography、autobiographical design、自己省察的デザイン、design ethnography。
- 優先データベース：CiNii、J-STAGE、ACM DL、SpringerLink、Google Scholar。日本語資料は出版社・研究室ページからも抽出する。
- ノートに既存枠組みや評価指標、批判点を整理し、Resonating Computerとの接続仮説をメモする。

## 5. Gaps and Open Questions
- For each domain, document what is established (validated findings) and what remains unresolved (measurement challenges, evaluation gaps, ethical or methodological limits).
- Note convergences or incompatibilities between technical AI pipelines and human sciences insights.

## Search Logistics
- Use scholarly databases (ACM DL, IEEE Xplore, SpringerLink, arXiv, Google Scholar) via command-line or manual browsing.
- Capture bibliographic details (authors, year, venue, key findings, limitations) in structured notes (see `survey/notes_*.md`).
- Prioritize peer-reviewed sources within the last 10 years, while keeping seminal works for historical framing.
- Tag each reference with domain labels (RS, AM, NI, HRI, PI) to support cross-sectional analysis.
